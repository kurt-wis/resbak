## Known design ↔ code gaps
- **Home diorama (reference match, 2026-10-18)**:
  - *Reference on disk.* The brief's reference image is now in the repo (`source_art/reference/home_target.webp`, Godot-ignored) and every Home prop is generated from a crop of it (`source_art/reference/home_crops/`, made by `tools/crop_home_reference.py`, which blends the UI stickers and the cast out of the boxes it keeps). The camera was solved numerically for that framing (eye 1.70 m at z 7.2, looking 2.4° down, fov 40); `--home-ref` draws the reference at 50 % over the frame to check the alignment by eye. It is a dev flag, off in a normal build.
  - *Store on a plinth.* The crop stops at the seated cast's heads, so the store model has no bottom: it stands with its base 0.80 m up (dropped 0.25 m on 2026-10-18 so its roof tyres enter the frame), and a procedural counter (`store_base.glb`) fills the band below it. Inside its own box the model's sign sits lower and further right than the reference's painted sign.
  - *Tricycle source.* The reference's left-hand parked tricycle is 74 % covered by the UI stickers and the right-hand one is about 40×80 px, so no clean crop exists; the tricycle model was made from a generated concept image instead (6 credits, `tools/meshy_api.py text-to-image`), and the right-hand one is a retexture of it.
  - *No soda logos.* The crate texture's light lettering was inpainted out in Blender at build time (`light_marks` in `tools/build_home_props.py`); the crates are plain red and blue.
  - *Wires.* Overhead cables are procedural catenary spans (`wire_span`), not Meshy: the generator reads them as flat strips. The pole model's baked cable strips are cut off at build time (`cut_radius`).
  - *Seamless hands (2026-10-19).* A gesture's end cue used to be lost when two gestures ended at the same instant (one method track holds one key per time), so a hero kept gesturing to the end of the clip and then snapped to the next one (Tutoy's hand hit 12 m/s at 39 s). Now each gesture has its own cue track, OneShot fades use a smoothstep curve (`gesture_fade_smooth`), Menu gestures are settled to the base idle's pose and trimmed to their scheduled `dur`, and the sit idles loop through a cross-fade. `HomeMotion` / `tools/qc_home_motion.gd` measure the hands at 60 Hz: worst hand speed per hero was 12.0 m/s and worst per-frame velocity change 12.2 m/s before, 8.9 and 2.5 after; the `home` suite fails above 8.0 / 3.0. Nathan's `Menu_CheerLeft` fade-in is the fastest remaining hand (about 7 m/s, a deliberate arm swing).
  - *Picker.* The default character is chosen in an overlay opened by the player card (spec § 6 puts a hero on Home's centre stage); the breakdance now plays inside the picker. The old arrows are gone.
  - *Master volume.* Defaults to 50 (spec § 10; it was 80). Existing `settings.cfg` files keep their saved value.
  - *English-first labels (spec deviation).* The reference labels Home's column PLAY / TRAINING / BACK TO TITLE and its tiles in English; the spec uses LARO / ENSAYO / LUMABAS. Both ship: the bigger line is English, the Filipino name is the smaller line under it, and the language setting still collapses it to one line (in single-language mode that line follows the setting).
  - *Meshy fallbacks.* `Menu_HandRub` was dropped. The tindera uses Wave One Hand plus a paper-fan prop. Trees (mango, coconut palm) and the graffiti wall are earlier Meshy output, decimated in Blender; they now stand only in the skyline. Skins for the cardboard UI are PIL-drawn (`tools/make_home_ui.py`), not Meshy. The action library has no hands-in-lap or leaning-forward seated idle (only the two `Chair_Sit_Idle` clips, both already in use), so the calmer seated variants are `Menu_ArmsCrossed`, `Menu_DozeOff` and `Menu_CheerLeft` from a 6-action preset batch.
  - *Palette remap off.* Meshy's store texture used to be remapped at runtime; the new textures already carry the reference palette, so `remap` is false per set piece and the shader only adds grain, grime, wrapped light and the filmic tonemap. Fog was cut from 0.008 to 0.0035 with the same pass (2026-10-18): at 0.008 the wooden house and the skyline slabs washed out to cream at their 8–13 m depth.
  - *Sky, blur and backdrop.* The sky is a custom `shaders/diorama_sky.gdshader` (gradient, low-sun glow, drifting clouds on a circle that closes every loop). Godot's Compatibility renderer has no depth of field, so the blur is a post-process quad on the camera (`shaders/diorama_dof.gdshader`, 24 depth-weighted taps, off with `dof_enabled`). A skyline of façade slabs (`backdrop` in `HomeDioramaData`) with PIL-generated textures fills the space behind the store.
  - *Street.* The road surface is a Meshy text-to-image decal (`assets/props/home/street_decal.png`, tiled every 10 m) carrying the chalk hopscotch and paint splats; it was levelled to the reference's warmer, darker street with a scratch tool (`renders/qc/patch_ground.py`), and its tile seams are visible as faint straight lines.
  - *Tricycle pass.* One tricycle crosses the side street at 3.2 m/s and is gone before the wrap; it reuses the synthesised engine and horn beds built for the old jeepney (`assets/audio/home/jeep_*.wav`).
  - *Schedule.* Thirteen long gestures with 0.5 s fades, a 2.5 gaze rate and a 30° head-yaw limit, replacing the earlier stack of short ones (which read as twitching). Held props (the palamig bag and the soda bottle) were dropped: the reference does not show them, and the grips were placeholders.
  - *Seat fit.* The stool is placed under each sitter's pelvis, not under the hero root, and scaled so the seat top sits `seat_pad` (0.06 m) below the pelvis; the `home` suite checks it within 3 cm and checks that no sole goes under the floor across the whole `Menu_SitIdle`.
  - *Cast order.* The two seated boys follow the picture's size order, not the roster's: JM (the big one) sits in the middle where the reference's towel kid sits, Nathan to his right in the thin one's seat. The reference's towel prop itself is not modelled — the held props were dropped.
  - *Audio.* All audio is synthesised by `tools/make_home_audio.py` (original, CC0). No recorded voices.
  - *Not verified.* Windowed frame rate was checked on this machine only; the gate runs headless, so the match to the reference (camera, prop placement, the street decal's tone) was eyeballed against a 50 % overlay and is not asserted by a test.
