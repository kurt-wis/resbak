## Skill commitment, readable indicators and fair ranges (2026-10-19)

Approved in two rounds (see [`decisions.md`](decisions.md)).

- **Nathan BUGA!** spends the whole 0.75 s inhale/spit planted: movement and actions stay locked, the aim direction is captured at the cast, and his facing, powder and 5 m / 60° cone all use that committed direction even if the stick turns mid-spit. The character keeps its 24 poses/s cadence. Movement and casting release together on the spit's last tick, so a press on that tick starts the next skill instead of being read as a recast.
- **Nene SIGAW!** is a fixed 60° sound fan 7 m deep (reduced from 12 m for balance): a visible front sweeps outward inside that fixed area, each hero it reaches is affected once (enemies stunned 0.5 s, teammates dash 3 m) and walls block the rays they cross.
- **No skill aim assist**: only the tag attack keeps its small gamepad assist. Every skill uses exactly the direction or point the player gave, the preview and the released cast are identical, and nothing selects, marks or pulls toward a target.
- **TAMARAW!!! is not aimable and has no direction tell**: it uses the same gesture as Tutoy's HARUROT! — hold the skill button, drag to CANCEL to drop it, release to charge — and then charges where the movement stick points, or where JM faces when the stick is centred, steering at its 90 deg/s limit. A mouse aim point never turns it, no arrow, lane or committed tell is drawn, and pressing S2 again still cancels the charge.
- **Shorter, fairer ranges**: HILA! 9 → 5 m, UBO! UBO! 9 → 6.5 m with the cloud radius 3.5 → 2.5 m, SIGAW! 12 → 7 m. Tag reach is arm-length: 1.0 m edge to edge (Tutoy 0.9 m), so bodies must be about 1.8 m apart instead of 2.0 m.
- **HILA! never hangs**: a reach that touches no teammate (or a wall) retracts on the tick after it opens, the hand has a hard flight-time timeout so no geometry case can leave it in the world, and the skinned arm returns in 0.15 s.
- **Stumble keeps its direction**: a failed tag (and Sabay) freezes the hero on the swing's direction for the whole fall. Aiming away no longer turns the body or the model, and the lock releases with the 0.5 s recovery.
- **Indicators**: aiming previews are private and show only the shape — no skill names, ranges, target rings or enemy highlights in the world. Comic ink under-stroke plus the skill's bright stroke over a restrained fill, with the colour alone carrying the state (skill colour, red for cancel, amber for blocked, cyan for max range). A committed cast publishes a matching tell for its commit window (SIGAW! and TALBOG! use their own in-world effect), then the real effect lands on the same area.
- **Spec deviations**: RESBAK.md § 4 describes SIGAW as a 12 m line that stops at the first hero, TAMARAW as steering by aim, UBO! as a 9 m / 3.5 m cloud, HILA! as a 9 m reach, lets Nathan move during the inhale, and gives 1.2 m tag reach. All are superseded here; the spec file itself is untouched.

## Known design ↔ code gaps
- **Home diorama (v0.1.2, 2026-10-18)**:
  - *Picker.* The default character is chosen in an overlay opened by the player card (spec § 6 puts a hero on Home's centre stage); the breakdance now plays inside the picker. The old arrows are gone.
  - *Master volume.* Defaults to 50 (spec § 10; it was 80). Existing `settings.cfg` files keep their saved value.
  - *Meshy fallbacks.* `Menu_HandRub` was dropped. The tindera uses Wave One Hand plus a paper-fan prop. The store comes from text-to-3D because the brief's reference image is not on disk. The trees (mango, coconut palm) and the graffiti wall are Meshy output, decimated in Blender. Skins for the cardboard UI are PIL-drawn (`tools/make_home_ui.py`), not Meshy.
  - *Store colours.* Meshy's store texture is baked saturated teal and orange; `diorama_set.gdshader` hue-remaps it to dusty mint and terracotta at runtime (grain, grime, wrapped light, filmic tonemap) instead of re-texturing.
  - *Sky, blur and backdrop.* The sky is a custom `shaders/diorama_sky.gdshader` (gradient, low-sun glow, drifting clouds on a circle that closes every loop). Godot's Compatibility renderer has no depth of field, so the blur is a post-process quad on the camera (`shaders/diorama_dof.gdshader`, 24 depth-weighted taps, off with `dof_enabled`). A skyline of façade slabs (`backdrop` in `HomeDioramaData`) and PIL-generated concrete ground textures (`tools/make_home_ground.py`) fill the space behind the store. The graffiti wall is a Meshy 3D model (`graffiti_wall.glb`), not a texture on a box.
  - *Jeepney visibility.* The store, wall and heroes cover most of the frame, so the jeepney is seen only near the right edge as it approaches the camera. It cruises at 3.6 m/s (about 4 s on screen) and is gone before the wrap.
  - *Schedule.* Nathan's `Answer` runs long, Nene's `SitDrink` is at 12.2 s, JM's second `SitDrink` and Tutoy's `TalkPassionate` were cut from the brief's schedule; Nene smiles instead of sipping at 43–46 s. The jeepney and walkers use a side street, not the main road.

  - *Audio.* All audio is synthesised by `tools/make_home_audio.py` (original, CC0). No recorded voices.
  - *Not verified.* Windowed frame rate was checked on this machine only; the gate runs headless, so the 3D backdrop itself is covered by `home` (builds, loop edges, turns) but not pixel-checked.
