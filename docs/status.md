## Known design ↔ code gaps
- **Home diorama (v0.1.2, 2026-10-18)**:
  - *Picker.* The default character is chosen in an overlay opened by the player card (spec § 6 puts a hero on Home's centre stage); the breakdance now plays inside the picker. The old arrows are gone.
  - *Master volume.* Defaults to 50 (spec § 10; it was 80). Existing `settings.cfg` files keep their saved value.
  - *Meshy fallbacks.* `Menu_HandRub` was dropped. The tindera uses Wave One Hand plus a paper-fan prop. The store comes from text-to-3D because the brief's reference image is not on disk. The trees (mango, coconut palm) and the graffiti wall are Meshy output, decimated in Blender. Skins for the cardboard UI are PIL-drawn (`tools/make_home_ui.py`), not Meshy.
  - *Store colours.* Meshy's store texture is baked saturated teal and orange; `diorama_set.gdshader` hue-remaps it to dusty mint and terracotta at runtime (grain, grime, wrapped light, filmic tonemap) instead of re-texturing.
  - *Sky, blur and backdrop.* The sky is a custom `shaders/diorama_sky.gdshader` (gradient, low-sun glow, drifting clouds on a circle that closes every loop). Godot's Compatibility renderer has no depth of field, so the blur is a post-process quad on the camera (`shaders/diorama_dof.gdshader`, 24 depth-weighted taps, off with `dof_enabled`). A skyline of façade slabs (`backdrop` in `HomeDioramaData`) and PIL-generated concrete ground textures (`tools/make_home_ground.py`) fill the space behind the store. The graffiti wall is a Meshy 3D model (`graffiti_wall.glb`), not a texture on a box.
  - *Jeepney visibility.* The store, wall and heroes cover most of the frame, so the jeepney is seen only near the right edge as it approaches the camera. It cruises at 3.6 m/s (about 4 s on screen) and is gone before the wrap.
  - *Schedule.* Nathan's `Answer` runs long, Nene's `SitDrink` is at 12.2 s, JM's second `SitDrink` and Tutoy's `TalkPassionate` were cut from the brief's schedule; Nene smiles instead of sipping at 43–46 s. The jeepney and walkers use a side street, not the main road.
  - *Held props.* Grips are placeholders (no per-hand tuning).
  - *Audio.* All audio is synthesised by `tools/make_home_audio.py` (original, CC0). No recorded voices.
  - *Not verified.* Windowed frame rate was checked on this machine only; the gate runs headless, so the 3D backdrop itself is covered by `home` (builds, loop edges, turns) but not pixel-checked.
- **Own-ring barrier removed (2026-10-17, deviation from spec § 2)**: a team is no longer barred from its own base ring. The pole is still solid and the capture rules are unchanged. The spec owner should confirm or restore it.
- **Spec gaps found 2026-10-17**: the spec has no Konstruksyon or sand-castle section; the hold-to-charge hampas is undocumented; the spec says a 0.15 s tag windup while the game uses 0.3 s.
- **Konstruksyon colliders by design**: the stone and gravel pile colliders sit inside their visible footprints (loose edge stones are walkable); the palm trunk has no collider.
- **Konstruksyon symmetry (2026-10-17)**: the two mid-field piles, both base sites and both Lila spawns now mirror about the yard's middle x = −2.0 within 0.05 m (`GravelEarthPile` 8.0 → 9.0 m, `BaseSite_Lila` 17.5 → 17.0 m, `Spawn_Lila_1/2` 17.5 → 16.5 m) and the `konstruksyon` suite checks bases, piles and both spawn pairs at 0.05 m. The two `JailLine_*` paths still differ in length (Dilaw ≈ 19.8 m, Lila ≈ 17.9 m) and keep the ±3 m suite tolerance.
- **MultiMesh property order (2026-10-17)**: `--import` logged 16 × `Instance count must be 0 to change the transform format` because the engine applies a scene's properties twice. Every one of the 24 `[sub_resource type="MultiMesh"]` blocks (`neighborhood_backdrop.tscn` 16, `construction_site.tscn` 8) now stores `instance_count = 0` before `transform_format = 1`; the import reports no errors and the runtime instance counts are unchanged.
- **Vehicle props (2026-10-17)**: `qc_asset.py` WARNs on normals (55–59 % flagged) for four of the five Meshy vehicles; they are open single-sided shells, which is normal Meshy output and renders correctly in game. The excavator body sits 1.4 m forward of its node on the rubble pile, as the old box model did.
- **v0.2 playtest changes are not in the spec yet** (team requests, not in `RESBAK.md`): Nene's SIGAW! / BAWAL! replace IYAK! / SUMBONG KITA! (spec § 4); Inday's HILA! replaces SAGIP!; the jail is a line with a sliding post instead of a yard; one fixed base per team instead of the site roulette; the slower pace and charged hampas; the announcer calls; desktop only (Windows). The spec should be updated by its owner. Nene's `Iyak` clip is now unused; HILA! reuses the `Sagip` clip.
- **BotNav sync** (fixed 2026-10-17): the navmesh used to report "synced" before its region joined the map, so early queries answered the origin and bots read every spot as unwalkable for the first frames. `BotNav.synced()` now also waits for the region to own map points.
- **Barrier bumps**: a 5v5 bots run still logs about 540 `barrier_bump` events in 19 s (bots pressing on base/jail barriers). Harmless, but bots waste time there.
- **Map size**: Barangay Court is about 39 × 29 m of play space; the spec says 56 × 32 m. Jails are lines now (v0.2), not the spec's 6 × 4 yard. Spawns and jail markers are derived by `MapLayout` (no `Spawn_*` / `JailMarker_*` nodes in the scene yet).
- **Facing**: the spec says the hero always faces the cursor. Heroes face their movement direction while running and turn to the aim when standing, tagging or casting; an aim fan under the local hero shows the lunge. Forward-only run clips look wrong when strafing. Team decision needed.
- **Slow walk cycles**: Nathan's Sneaky Walk and JM's Proud Strut have short strides, so at the spec walk speed they play about 8.9× and 4.4×. Walking only happens on a gamepad (15–50 % tilt). Fix: a faster Meshy walk preset (3 credits each) or another walk.
- **JM Talbog_Slam**: his fist dips up to 12 cm under the floor at impact (covered by the dust ring).
- **Assists and Kidlat** are not tracked yet, so those badges never appear.
- **Skill visuals are code-built** (CPUParticles, simple meshes). No authored VFX textures or sounds yet; `Audio.play()` names are placeholders until audio lands.
- **Legacy dev code**: `scripts/runtime/` (HeroController, HeroRoster, character select, Batang Hamog prototype, movement test) is kept for reference and the `legacy` suite; the match does not use it. `jm_botsog.glb` is unused.
- The `scripts/tests/legacy/verify_*.gd` checks fail against the current map; they are historical.
