# Character Dossier: Nene

## Overview & Identity
- **Name**: Nene
- **Archetype / Primary Role**: **Deception**
- **Secondary Specialty**: **Rescue**
- **Vibe & Concept**: The quick-witted, sneaky little sister who knows every trick in the book. Nene manipulates opponent perceptions, fakes alarms, summons weeping decoys, and slips past defenders unnoticed to execute clutch jailbreaks.

---

## Combat & Ability Specifications

### Active Skill 1: IYAK!
- **Category**: Decoy Spawn & Camouflage
- **Mechanics**:
  - Nene halts and bursts into an exaggerated, dramatic theatrical crying fit.
  - An animated crying decoy is left at her exact position, loudly drawing audio-visual attention.
  - Simultaneously, the real Nene enters a semi-transparent camouflage state for 3.5 seconds, becoming difficult to see against the court environment.
- **Rules & Limitations**:
  - The crying decoy attracts the gaze of opponents and minimap alerts.
  - The real Nene remains physically taggable; executing an attack, tagging, or casting another skill instantly ends her camouflage.

### Active Skill 2: SUMBONG KITA!
- **Category**: Audio-Visual Diversion / False Intel
- **Mechanics**:
  - Nene targets a designated area on the court within a 15-meter range and throws a false enemy intrusion ping.
  - The target location plays distinct audio cues of rapid running footsteps and displays a simulated threat ping on opponent screens, mimicking an incoming attack or rescue attempt.
- **Constraints & Counterplay**:
  - The alert cannot mimic an actual round-ending base capture or score announcement.
  - Observant players can notice subtle visual discrepancies in the ping icon and footstep pattern to recognize the ruse.

### Passive Trait: SALING-PUSA
- **Category**: Stealth / Audio Dampening
- **Mechanics**:
  - Derived from the Filipino cultural concept of *Saling-Pusa* (the playful younger participant whom others overlook).
  - After remaining out of direct line of sight or enemy pursuit for 3.0 seconds, Nene's running footsteps become completely silent.
- **Detection**:
  - Saling-Pusa immediately ends if an enemy comes within a 3.0-meter close-range radius, or if Nene casts a skill or initiates a tag.
