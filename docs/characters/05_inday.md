# Character Dossier: Inday

## Overview & Identity
- **Name**: Inday
- **Archetype / Primary Role**: **Rescue**
- **Secondary Specialty**: **Deception**
- **Vibe & Concept**: The supportive, sharp-eyed older sister who organizes the neighborhood play and refuses to let any friend get left behind in jail. Inday excels at team acceleration, daring long-range jailbreaks, and orchestrated escapes.

---

## Combat & Ability Specifications

### Active Skill 1: IHIP!
- **Category**: Directional Tailwind Channel
- **Mechanics**:
  - Inday plants her feet and channels a sustained, visible stream of wind in a chosen forward direction (12.0-meter length, 2.5-meter width).
  - Any ally running inside the wind tunnel receives a continuous +30% movement speed boost.
  - Enemies can traverse the wind corridor freely, but gain zero speed benefit.
- **Vulnerabilities & Channel Rules**:
  - The wind tunnel remains active only while Inday channels (up to 4.5 seconds maximum).
  - While channeling, Inday can aim the wind stream, but cannot move, tag, or cast another skill.
  - She remains fully vulnerable to tags. Any tag or crowd-control hit immediately interrupts the channel and dissipates the wind corridor.

### Active Skill 2: SAGIP!
- **Category**: Extended Reach Jailbreak & Runner Decoy
- **Mechanics**:
  - Inday enters a focused rescue stance, temporarily extending her rescue-touch range by +2.5 meters for 2.0 seconds.
  - This allows her to touch the outermost tip of an enemy prisoner chain from outside the guards' immediate reach.
  - Upon a successful rescue touch, a running illusion decoy of Inday spawns and sprints in a chosen direction with audible fake footsteps to lure jail guards away.
- **Decoy Behavior**:
  - The decoy cannot tag opponents, rescue additional prisoners, or capture a base pad.
  - The decoy instantly pops and vanishes upon making physical contact with any enemy player.

### Passive Trait: TAKBO NA!
- **Category**: Post-Jailbreak Team Surge
- **Mechanics**:
  - "Run now!" Whenever Inday successfully triggers a jailbreak by touching an imprisoned teammate, Inday and all newly freed teammates receive an immediate +25% sprint speed surge for 2.5 seconds.
  - Activates exactly once per jailbreak event, regardless of how many prisoners are linked in the chain.
- **Stacking & Balance**:
  - The speed surge does not stack with the speed boost from **Ihip!**.
  - The passive provides a speed burst to escape, but does not grant tag immunity beyond the standard base jailbreak grace period.
